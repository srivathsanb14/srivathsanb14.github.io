About

I am a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University. I joined the Mechanical and AI Lab (MAIL Lab) in Fall 2026, advised by Prof. Amir Barati Farimani. My research focuses on physics-informed machine learning that combines molecular language, graph, and 3D structure representations for therapeutic and materials discovery in data-limited settings.

I received an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence Engineering (Chemical Engineering) from CMU in 2024 and a B.Tech in Chemical Engineering from IIT Madras in 2023. Earlier research experience includes work at the University of Alberta (MITACS Globalink) and IIT Madras. See my publications and experience and my CV.
